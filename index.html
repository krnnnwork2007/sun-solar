Common worries

Going solar should not feel confusing.

Most people ask the same six things before they call. Here is what we help you sort out first.

Which system size is right for my bill?

We read your bill and your daytime usage, then suggest the smallest system that covers it.

Is my roof suitable for solar?

We check shade, space and direction on site before we quote anything.

How does net metering work?

Extra units you send to the grid are credited against what you use later. You pay for the net.

What subsidy can I get?

Homes can apply under PM Surya Ghar. We confirm the current amount and help with the form.

What paperwork is needed?

Usually a recent bill, ID and address proof, and roof details. We give you a clear list.

Who looks after it later?

The same team that installed it. Cleaning and checkups are part of what we offer.

How big a system do I need?

As a rule of thumb, 1 kW makes around 120 units a month in Delhi. Divide your monthly units by 120 for a starting size. We adjust it for your roof space and shade.

Is there a government subsidy?

Homes can apply under the PM Surya Ghar rooftop solar scheme. Amounts and rules change, so we confirm the current figure when we quote and help with the application.

How does net metering work?

Your system supplies your home first. Extra units go to the grid and are credited to you. When you use more than you make, those credits are used, and you pay only for the net units.

Will it work during a power cut?

A standard on-grid system switches off when the grid goes down, for safety. If you want power during a cut, choose a hybrid system with a battery.

Do the panels need cleaning?

Yes. Dust builds up quickly in Delhi and reduces output. Clean them regularly, or ask us about a service visit.

What documents do I need?

Usually a recent electricity bill, ID and address proof, and details showing the roof is yours to use. We confirm the exact list for your case.

How long does installation take?

It depends on system size and approvals. We give you an expected timeline in the proposal.

Do you cover areas outside Badli?

We are based in Badli and work across North Delhi. Call us with your location and we will confirm.
